### Calibration of the model
The 3–5% figure is a population-level statistic that emerges from the convolution of three independent things: the TCR repertoire distribution, the peptide presentation landscape in the thymus, and the selection thresholds. This model has independent control over all three. The koff<sub>base</sub> log-normal distribution represents the TCR repertoire. The reference ligand koff values represent the peptide landscape. The thresholds are anchored to the OT-I data.
